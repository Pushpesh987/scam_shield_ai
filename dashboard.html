Simple rules to remember before answering unknown callers

Modern scammers rely on panic and urgency. Memorize these three Golden Rules to keep yourself and your family immune.

Golden Rule #1 • App Downloads

Real banks never ask for remote screen sharing

Never install AnyDesk, TeamViewer, or QuickSupport if instructed by someone on a phone call. These give criminals total view of your banking passwords and OTPs.

Golden Rule #2 • Digital Arrest Reality

Police officers NEVER arrest people via WhatsApp video

No genuine police force, CBI, or Enforcement Directorate operates courts or issues warrants over video calls. Any caller demanding you remain on video is an extortionist.
