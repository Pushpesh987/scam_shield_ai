Common Scam Modus Operandi & The Ground Truth

Scammers use extreme emotional pressure, legal panic, and artificial urgency. Read these clear facts so you or your parents can never be deceived.

gavel
High Frequency

"Digital Arrest" (Fake Police / CBI / ED / Customs)

Scammer's False Claim:

"An illegal courier parcel containing contraband was intercepted in your name. You must stay on Skype/WhatsApp video in private for 6 hours under 'Digital Arrest' or face SWAT arrest."

verified The Ground Truth

There is no such law as 'Digital Arrest' in the Indian Penal Code. Law enforcement agencies (CBI, ED, State Police) NEVER conduct interrogations, make arrests, or demand fund transfers over video call.

warning Red Flag: Forbidding you from talking to children or family.
bolt
SMS Threat

Electricity Bill Disconnection Panic

Scammer's False Claim:

"Dear consumer, your electricity will be disconnected tonight at 9:30 PM due to unpaid balance. Immediately dial our Discom officer at 98xxxxxxx or download this APK."

verified The Ground Truth

Government power utilities never provide personal 10-digit mobile numbers in SMS alerts and never ask consumers to download remote-desktop apps (AnyDesk, QuickSupport) to pay bills.

warning Red Flag: Asking to pay a ₹10 "token charge" to test connection.
account_balance_wallet
OTP Theft

Bank KYC Freeze & Aadhaar Expiry

Scammer's False Claim:

"Your bank account or SIM card will be deactivated within 24 hours. Update your Aadhaar KYC right now by sharing the 6-digit confirmation code sent to your mobile."

verified The Ground Truth

No bank, telecom provider, or government entity will ever ask for a one-time password (OTP) over an unsolicited phone call. OTPs are meant for authorising debits, never for verifying status.

warning Red Flag: Any request to read back an SMS code received on your phone.
